### Made for Wrenn

Generated by a small script (filtered noise and sine sweeps), not recorded and not taken from anyone. Released by the Wrenn project under CC0 1.0.

| File | What it is |
| - | - |
| sfx/pop-3.wav | Short sine with a fast pitch drop (bubble pop) |
| sfx/whoosh-1.wav | Filtered noise sweep, left to right, peak at 0.30 s |
| sfx/whoosh-2.wav | Longer, lower filtered noise sweep, peak at 0.48 s |
| sfx/impact-2.wav | Deep sine drop with a short click on top (sub hit) |
| sfx/riser-1.wav | 2 s noise and tone riser, ends on its peak |
| sfx/riser-2.wav | 1 s noise and tone riser, ends on its peak |
